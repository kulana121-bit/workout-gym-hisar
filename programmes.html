The programmes

Pick your goal.

Descriptions below are how the sessions run at The Workout Gym. Availability is confirmed by the gym — ask at the desk which sessions are running this week.

Programme 01

Weight loss

Built around cardio blocks, circuits and steady strength work. The aim is simple: burn more than you take in, keep the muscle you already have, and make it repeatable in a normal week.

  • Cardio and machine work scaled to your current fitness
  • Circuits that mix strength and conditioning in one block
  • Simple diet guidance that fits a normal working day
  • A coach on the floor to keep the form honest
Suitable for
Beginners, returners, and anyone who wants a plan instead of guesswork.
Format
Cardio + circuits + strength, coached on the floor.

Programme 02

Muscle building

Progressive resistance training for size: controlled reps, sensible loads and a split you can recover from. The numbers move because the plan moves — not because you train to failure every day.

  • Barbell and machine work for every major muscle group
  • Progressive loading so the numbers keep moving
  • Volume and rest matched to your recovery
  • Coach-checked technique on the big lifts
Suitable for
Lifters chasing visible size, and beginners ready to learn the basics properly.
Format
Split or full-body sessions with a written progression.
Enquire about muscle building Meet the coaching

Programme 03

Strength training

Squat, press, pull. Racks, barbells and plates with someone watching the bar path while you work up to a heavier set.

  • Power racks, benches and a full plate stack
  • Squat, bench, deadlift and overhead work
  • Technique coaching before the loads go up
  • Simple progression you can track each week
Suitable for
Anyone who wants to be genuinely strong — from a first squat to a heavier working set.
Format
Barbell-focused sessions in the racks, supervised.
Enquire about strength training See membership

Programme 04

Personal training

One coach, one plan. Your body, your schedule, your goal — with the diet side handled by the same person who counts your reps.

  • A written plan for your goal and your week
  • One-to-one attention on form and load
  • Diet guidance alongside the training
  • Cardio, weight training and CrossFit-style sessions
Suitable for
People who want accountability, or who are training around an old injury.
Format
One-to-one sessions booked with a coach. Charges are confirmed by the gym.

Programme 05

General fitness

For staying active rather than chasing numbers: balanced full-body sessions that leave you better than you came in — fitter, moving well, sleeping properly.

  • Full-body sessions mixing strength and cardio
  • Scales to any starting level, including complete beginners
  • No pressure to chase numbers you do not care about
  • Space to train at your own pace, with a coach nearby
Suitable for
Anyone who wants to feel fitter and stay injury-free while doing it.
Format
Full-body sessions, two to four days a week.

How it works

From first visit to a plan.

  1. Step 01

    Send a trial request

    Tell us your goal and the day that suits you. Nothing is booked until the gym confirms the slot.

  2. Step 02

    Walk in

    Come in during opening hours with training shoes and a water bottle. A coach shows you around the floor.

  3. Step 03

Questions

Training FAQs.

I have never trained before. Can I still join?
Yes. Tell the coach it is your first week and they will take you through the main machines and the setup. Start light — technique first, then load.
How many days a week should I train?
Most members train three to five days. Beginners usually do best with three sessions, full-body or a simple split, with a rest day in between.
Does the gym give diet plans?
Simple food guidance comes with the weight-loss and personal-training programmes. It is general guidance, not medical advice — for a medical condition, please follow your doctor’s instructions first.
What equipment is on the floor?
Power racks and barbells, a full dumbbell wall, machine stations for every muscle group and a cardio row for warm-ups and conditioning.
What should I bring on the first day?
Training shoes, comfortable clothes, a towel and a water bottle. That is all.
