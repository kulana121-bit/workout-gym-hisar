Not a gym for everyone. A gym for anyone serious about showing up.

The Workout Gym sits on Kaimri–Mangali Road in Sector 15: one floor for strength, one for cardio, and space for the functional work in between. Walk in during opening hours and a coach will show you around.

  • Open 17 hours a day, Monday to Saturday — early morning to late evening.
  • Coaches on the floor, not behind a desk — form, technique and a plan that fits you.
  • Personal training available for cardio, weight training and CrossFit-style sessions.
  • Train a day first. Request a free trial and decide after you have seen the floor.

Coaching

A coach on the floor.
Not behind a desk.

Coaches are on the floor during opening hours, so someone can fix a setup, spot a lift and tell you what to do next. Personal training is available for cardio, weight training and CrossFit-style sessions.

  • Technique checks on the main lifts, with a coach on the floor.
  • Personal training with a written plan and diet guidance.
  • Long opening hours — 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
