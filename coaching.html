On the floor

What coaching looks like here.

Personal training is available for cardio, weight training and CrossFit-style sessions. Even on a standard membership, the coaches on the floor will correct your setup and tell you what to do next.

  • Technique checks on the main lifts, on the floor.
  • Personal training with a written plan and diet guidance.
  • Cardio, weight training and CrossFit-style sessions, depending on your goal.
  • Long opening hours — 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.

How it works

A plan that moves with you.

  1. Step 01

    Talk it through

    Tell the coach your goal, your week and anything that hurts. That is where the plan starts.

  2. Step 02

    Learn the lifts

    Setup, range of motion and breathing on the movements your programme uses.

  3. Step 03

    Train the plan

    Reps, sets and loads written down, with the diet side kept simple and realistic.

  4. Step 04

    Reassess

    Progress is reviewed and the plan is adjusted — heavier, lighter or different, as needed.

The coaching team.
