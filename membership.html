Questions

Membership FAQs.

How much does membership cost?
Fees are set by the gym and confirmed at the desk or over the phone. Ask for the current plan sheet when you visit, or send a request and the team will tell you today’s rates for monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and annual plans.
Can I try the gym before I pay?
Yes — request a free trial and train a session with the coach on the floor. Nothing is charged and nothing is booked until the gym confirms your slot.
Do you offer a monthly plan?
Yes. Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and annual plans are listed above. The longer plans simply give you a longer access period — the gym confirms which one makes sense for you.
Is personal training included in membership?
Personal training is a separate service booked with a coach, so it is not included in the plan fees. Ask at the desk for coach availability and charges.
Are there any membership documents to fill in?
The gym will take your basic details at the desk when you join. Bring an ID and a mobile number you answer — everything else is done in person.
What are the timings?
Monday to Saturday, 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM. The gym is closed on Sundays.
