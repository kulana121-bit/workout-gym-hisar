Practical

Before you visit.

Do I need to book before coming in?
No. You can walk in any time between 5:00 AM and 10:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. If you want a coach to keep a slot aside for you, send a trial request first.
What happens after I send a trial request?
The request reaches the gym with your name, number, goal and preferred day and time. The team confirms the slot with you before you turn up. Nothing is booked until they reply.
How do I find the gym?
The Workout Gym is on Kaimri–Mangali Road in Sector 15, near Soni Burrn Hospital, Hisar. Use the map above or tap “Get directions” for turn-by-turn navigation. The Google Plus Code for the gym is 4PPC+9G Hisar.
Is there a phone number I can call?
The gym’s phone number is being confirmed before it is published here, so this site does not show a placeholder number. Send a request through the form and the team will call you back, or walk in during opening hours.
Can I park near the gym?
Parking is on the street around Sector 15. Ask at the desk when you arrive and the staff will point you to the nearest spot.
